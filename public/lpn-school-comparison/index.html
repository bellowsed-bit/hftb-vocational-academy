Goodwin University — Practical Nursing

LocationOne Riverside Drive, East Hartford, CT 06118
Program lengthAbout 16 months full-time after acceptance
Current urgent dateOfficial TEAS score must be received by Friday, August 28, 2026
TEASMinimum published score: 54.7%

Prerequisites

  • MATH 125 — Mathematical Applications for the Health Sciences
  • ENG 101 — English Composition
  • COM 105 — Interpersonal Communications
  • BIO 101 or an eligible higher biology course
  • Minimum C grade required in prerequisite courses

Cost & funding

Goodwin publishes institutional tuition rates rather than one simple all-in Practical Nursing total. The exact cost for this client should be obtained through a written personalized cost/financial-aid package after transfer credits are reviewed. Goodwin identifies FAFSA, grants, scholarships, loans, work-study, Veteran support, and SNAP Employment & Training among potential resources.

Immediate action

  • If a qualifying TEAS score already exists, send the official score immediately.
  • If TEAS has not been taken, call admissions immediately and ask whether an on-campus testing option can still satisfy the August 28 receipt deadline.
  • Request transfer-credit evaluation, exact tuition/fees, and amount due before class begins.

Griffin Hospital School of Allied Health Careers — Practical Nursing

Location1186 New Haven Road, Naugatuck, CT 06770
Program length14 months
Next startNot clearly published in current public material — must be confirmed directly
Entrance examHESI A2

Program structure

Griffin's Practical Nursing program combines classroom/virtual instruction, skills laboratories, simulation, and clinical experiences. It is a Connecticut-approved Practical Nursing pathway.

Admission items currently identified

  • Completed application
  • High-school diploma or equivalent
  • Admissions interview
  • HESI A2 entrance examination
  • Health screening and drug/toxicology requirements
  • Application fee is listed in the current application materials

Cost & funding

A current all-in 2026 Practical Nursing tuition total is not clearly posted on the public site. Ask for a written total covering tuition, fees, books, uniforms, clinical supplies, testing, health requirements, and other mandatory expenses.

Funding caution: do not count Futuro as a confirmed Griffin LPN funding source unless Griffin confirms it in writing. Ask specifically whether the Practical Nursing program is currently eligible for The WorkPlace, WIOA, or Health CareeRx funding.

Questions to ask Griffin

  • When does the next Practical Nursing cohort start?
  • What is the last date to complete admission?
  • What is the full 14-month cost?
  • What HESI A2 score is required?
  • Is the PN program currently fundable through The WorkPlace/WIOA/Health CareeRx?

CT State Community College — Norwalk Practical Nursing

Location188 Richards Avenue, Norwalk, CT 06854
Program length4 semesters / 1,500 nursing hours
Application windowJune 1 through October 1, 2026
Program startSpring 2027 Norwalk cohort
TEASMinimum 55.4%; score must meet the published recency rules
Published CT resident tuition$192 per credit plus applicable service/program fees

Required prerequisite courses

  • English Composition
  • General Psychology
  • Lifespan Development
  • Cell Biology with Organ Systems, or an allowable published substitute such as qualifying A&P coursework
  • Minimum C grade required

Cost

CT State is currently the strongest low-cost option among the four schools. For Connecticut residents, the published tuition rate is $192 per credit plus fees. The Practical Nursing packet also identifies additional program costs such as clinical requirements, testing, books, equipment, uniforms, background/toxicology screening, and licensing/NCLEX-related expenses.

Immediate action

  • Get all transcripts evaluated against the four prerequisites.
  • Do not assume Sophia Learning courses will transfer; obtain an official evaluation.
  • Determine current TEAS status before retesting because CT State's published rules use the most recent qualifying score.
  • Submit the application before October 1 even while remaining documentation is being finalized, if permitted under the current instructions.
