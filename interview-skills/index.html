PRESSURE MODE

Trying to prove yourself

  • Rushes answers
  • Searches for approval
  • Uses canned language
  • Makes the body smaller
  • Treats every question like a test

PROFESSIONAL MODE

Exploring mutual fit

  • Pauses before answering
  • Shares facts honestly
  • Connects experience to purpose
  • Uses grounded, open posture
  • Treats the interview as a conversation
